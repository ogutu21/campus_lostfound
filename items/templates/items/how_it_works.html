How it works

Lost something on campus, or found something that isn't yours? Post it here, so the right person can get it back. It takes about a minute.

I lost something
  1. Post it Choose I lost something. Say what it is, where you think you lost it, and add a photo if you have one.
  2. Check the Found items Search the home page with a word like "phone" or "library". Someone may already have handed yours in.
  3. Wait for a message If someone finds it, they message you privately. You get an email, and a number shows on Messages.
  4. Mark it resolved Once you have it back, open My posts and tap Mark resolved so people stop looking.
I found something
  1. Post it Choose I found something, say where you found it, and add a photo. Keep the item safe, or hand it to campus security.
  2. Write a verification question Ask something only the real owner would know, such as "What is on the lock screen?". Never put the answer in the description.
  3. Review claims When someone says it's theirs, you see their answer under Claims. You decide: Approve or Reject.
  4. Arrange the handover Approving opens a private chat with them. Agree on a busy place, hand it over, then mark the post resolved.
I want to claim a found item
  1. Tap Claim Open the found item that looks like yours and tap Claim.
  2. Confirm you're a student (first time only) Enter your student registration number once. It must be on the campus list, and each number works for one person only.
  3. Answer the question Answer the finder's question as precisely as you can.
  4. Wait for the finder You get an email when they decide. If you're approved, a private chat opens so you can arrange to collect it.

Stay safe

  • Meet somewhere busy on campus, like the security office or the library desk.
  • Never pay anyone to get your item back, and never send money for "delivery".
  • Don't post private details in public comments. Use the private chat instead.
  • Check an item in person before you hand it over or take it, especially phones and ID cards.
  • If a post looks fake, rude or wrong, tap Report on it.

Questions

Do I need a registration number to join?

No. Sign in with any Google account to browse, post, comment and send messages. The registration number is asked only the first time you try to claim an item.

Why do you ask for my registration number?

To make sure the person claiming an item is a student at this campus. Each number can be linked to one account only.

I typed my number wrong too many times.

After five wrong tries verification locks, to stop people guessing other students' numbers. {% if contact %}Email {{ contact }} and we'll unlock you. {% else %}Ask the site admin to unlock you.{% endif %}

How long do posts stay up?

After 30 days we email you to ask if it's still unresolved. Tap Renew to keep it up for another 30 days, or Mark resolved if it's sorted.

My post disappeared. What happened?

If a reminder goes unanswered for 7 days, the post comes down from the public list. Nothing is deleted. Open My posts, go to the Expired tab, and tap Put it back up.

Who can read my private messages?

Only you and the other person. Site administrators can technically open messages, and only do so to look into a report or a problem.

What happens to my photos?

They are shrunk so pages load fast on mobile data. Hidden location details that phones add to photos are removed.

Can I change or delete a post?

Yes. Open My posts to edit, mark resolved, or delete any of your posts.

How do I stop the emails?

Open Settings and untick the email box. That stops comment, claim and message emails. You will still get the occasional notice about your own old posts, and everything is always on the site.

Can I use it like a phone app?

Yes. Open Settings and tap Install the app. On iPhone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen.
